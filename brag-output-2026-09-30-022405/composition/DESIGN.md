# Concierge.Ai Video Design

## Style Prompt
Quiet premium hotel welcome derived from the existing Concierge.Ai guest interface. Keep the mobile UI recognizable, calm, and useful. Use pale cool-neutral surfaces, navy structure, and a restrained teal accent; leave generous breathing room around the phone. Use serif display headlines with a clean sans-serif interface. Transitions glide and hold rather than snap or flash.

## Colors
- Canvas: `#fbfcfe`
- Soft surface: `#f6f7f9`
- Secondary surface: `#ffffff`
- Primary text: `#111827`
- Secondary text: `#667085`
- Brand navy: `#172033`
- Brand teal: `#0f766e`
- Hairline/border: `#dfe5ee`

## Typography
- Display: Iowan Old Style, Baskerville, Times New Roman, serif
- Interface/body: Geist Sans, Inter, system sans-serif
- Scene headline: regular/medium serif; phone UI: source-like sans weights.

## Motion
- 0.35–0.65s entrances, soft easing, no flashing.
- Keep the phone mostly steady; use gentle lateral movement and a little depth.
- Answer and CTA appear sequentially with enough settled time to read.
- Transitions remain soft crossfades or calm slides.

## What NOT to Do
- No neon/cyan gradients or generic AI particles.
- No fake Wi-Fi authentication success or implication that Concierge.Ai opens the internet.
- No real hotel or guest data; sample content must be labeled fictional.
- No flashing text, waveform graphics, or aggressive motion.
