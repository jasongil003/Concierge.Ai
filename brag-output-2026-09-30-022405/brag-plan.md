# Brag Plan: Concierge.Ai

## What is this app?
Concierge.Ai is an on-prem, AI-powered digital concierge for hotel guests, designed around the guest Wi-Fi journey and property-specific information and service workflows.

## The angle
The guest experience can begin at the Wi-Fi welcome. Show one believable, useful moment from a hotel stay: join the guest network, open the concierge, ask a simple property question, and get an answer grounded in that property's own information. The phone UI is based on the real guest app; the sample hotel and exchange are fictional demo content. Keep the gateway boundary accurate: ANTlabs controls guest authentication and internet access.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“The stay starts on Wi-Fi.” A phone is already in the guest's hand; a small “Hotel guest Wi-Fi” connection chip settles beside the app. The line earns the reveal without suggesting Concierge.Ai itself authenticates the guest or opens internet access.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Reveal the app in its real pale guest UI: hotel identity, “Good evening,” “Your stay,” quick actions, and bottom navigation.
- Tap Concierge and type “Where can I get breakfast?” into the actual style of the guest composer.
- Show a short demo response tied to fictional property info: “Breakfast is served in the Garden Room. Want the menu?” Then surface a “Browse dining” action.

## Outro / punchline
Concierge.Ai wordmark with the source claim: “On-prem, AI-powered hotel concierge.” Supporting line: “Designed around guest Wi-Fi.” No claim that the prototype has a validated production gateway handoff.

## User flow worth showing
Hotel guest Wi-Fi entry → open the guest concierge and ask a property question → get a property-informed answer and a useful follow-up action. The video demonstrates the intended guest experience, not production authentication; ANTlabs remains authoritative for authentication and internet access.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet premium hotel welcome, with one crisp phone interaction
- Interpretation: restrained motion, warm light, and a short readable product journey; the guest interface carries the story instead of feature-list copy.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#fbfcfe` to `#eef2f7` in the guest app shell, with a `#f6f7f9` surface
- Accent: `#0f766e` teal and `#172033` navy
- Text: `#111827` primary and `#667085` secondary
- Display font: Iowan Old Style / Baskerville / Times New Roman serif stack from `styles.css`
- Body font: Geist Sans / Inter / system sans stack from `styles.css`
- Strongest visual element: the guest phone UI, with its restrained pale surface, teal-and-navy hotel mark, serif welcome heading, privacy-first conversation, and compact quick actions.

## Share copy (draft)
Concierge.Ai brings an on-prem digital concierge into the hotel Wi-Fi welcome: property-informed answers and guest services, in one calm mobile experience.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, understated business bed with a few gentle interface accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, warm and measured
- Music treatment: begin at 0s at low-to-moderate level; hold under the UI; soft fade over the final second.
- Music cue guidance: bundled vol-11 preset, 114.84 BPM. Major visual moments may land near 3.70s (product UI), 12.65s (answer), and 17.91s (wordmark); those three strong cues are optional. Use the 0.52s beat grid only for small non-text accents. Keep the typed question readable and do not reveal words at beat speed.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let an existing teal glow or the phone's shadow breathe slightly with the track. No audio-reactive text scale or waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse and soft, with one tap/click and one warm reveal accent
- Audio-coupled moments: the Concierge tap, the send action, and the answer settling in
- Restraint rule: keep the mix hospitable and quiet; sound should support the guest's action, not sell a fictional hotel.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Wi-Fi welcome — 3.7s
A close, elegant phone silhouette with a “Hotel guest Wi-Fi” label beside the app. Main line: “The stay starts on Wi-Fi.” Do not show an authentication status, gateway handoff, or imply internet access is granted by Concierge.Ai.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the Wi-Fi label appears, then the headline settles and holds.
Audio intent: music arrives softly; one subtle confirmation accent.
Audio-coupled idea: lock the headline/phone reveal near the 1.60s strong cue; let the chip settle on a quieter beat.
Music: warm low-volume bed.
Transition mood: soft crossfade with a small lateral drift → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — A familiar guest home — 3.7s
Reveal a polished recreation of the real guest home from `app/static/index.html` and `app/static/styles.css`: fictional property name “Harbor House,” “Good evening,” “Your stay,” “Quick actions,” and Home / Explore / Requests / My Stay / Concierge navigation. Callout: “A concierge, right where the stay begins.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — property mark and welcome arrive first; the quick-action row follows; Concierge becomes the focus last.
Audio intent: warm and confident, with one restrained reveal sound.
Audio-coupled idea: major product UI reveal near 3.70s; buttons arrive slowly enough to read.
Music: steady, understated.
Transition mood: clean slide/crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Ask — 4.9s
The guest taps Concierge and types “Where can I get breakfast?” in the real guest composer style. The question remains fully visible; no rapid per-character text flash.
Sequential/interaction: yes — tap Concierge, composer focus, question typed, send tap.
Audio intent: one quiet UI click; keyboard ticks only if they remain gentle and sparse.
Audio-coupled idea: tap sound at interaction start; optional beat-grid accents for the send icon, never for the words.
Music: same bed, no build that competes with the question.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — A useful answer — 5.1s
Show the user bubble, then the response: “Breakfast is served in the Garden Room. Want the menu?” A compact “Browse dining” action arrives below. The hotel, guest, and operational detail are fictional demo content, clearly labeled “Sample property info” in the phone UI.
Sequential/interaction: yes — user question, assistant answer, and dining action settle in that order.
Audio intent: gentle, reassuring answer reveal; avoid an overblown AI sound.
Audio-coupled idea: answer lands near the 12.65s strong cue; dining action follows at a readable interval.
Music: unchanged, supporting the response.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Concierge.Ai — 2.6s
The screen widens to the wordmark and the actual product claim: “On-prem, AI-powered hotel concierge.” Smaller support line: “Designed around guest Wi-Fi.” Hold on the logo for the poster-worthy finish.
Sequential/interaction: yes — wordmark first, claim second, support line last.
Audio intent: one soft final accent; music tails out gently.
Audio-coupled idea: wordmark lands near 17.91s; final copy holds to the end.
Music: fade during the last second.
Transition mood: soft fade to a calm final frame.

**Music mood for this video:** warm, understated, polished.
**Audio summary:** A light music bed moves with one clear guest action and a gentle answer reveal, then resolves under the product name.

1. **What is the app?** An on-prem hotel guest concierge combining property information and service workflows in a Wi-Fi-aware mobile experience.

3. **Visual hook?** The guest UI's pale, softly lit shell, serif welcome type, and navy/teal hotel mark.
4. **What should be shown?** The actual-style guest home, composer, chat exchange, and dining action.
5. **Shortest satisfying video?** 20 seconds allows the guest flow to read without rushing.
6. **Tone?** Polished; a quiet premium hotel welcome.
7. **Audio?** Warm, low-volume music and sparse, soft UI cues.
8. **Share caption?** “Concierge.Ai brings an on-prem digital concierge into the hotel Wi-Fi welcome: property-informed answers and guest services, in one calm mobile experience.”
9. **User flow?** Guest Wi-Fi entry → ask about the property → receive a property-informed answer and follow-up action.

**Fictional demo data:** “Harbor House,” the guest Wi-Fi label, Garden Room, and the breakfast exchange are fabricated specifically for this video. No customer, guest, account, or hotel records are used.
