# Storyboard — Concierge.AI

**Strategy:** This video tells hotel operators that guest Wi-Fi can become the first step into a smoother, more connected stay.  
**Format:** 1920×1080, 20 seconds.  
**Audio:** Warm English voiceover; no music or extra sound effects.  
**Style basis:** `DESIGN.md` and the live Concierge.AI guest-interface capture.  
**Run shape:** Automation, no review checkpoint; this storyboard is the production brief.

## Asset audit

## Beat plan

### Beat 1 — More than a connection · 0.00–4.00s

**VO:** “Your hotel's Wi-Fi can do more than connect. It can start a smoother stay.”

**Concept:** The guest's first digital touchpoint opens like a warm welcome rather than a utility screen. A Wi-Fi connection chip introduces the actual Concierge.AI guest page, making the site's interface the hero instead of an abstract AI symbol.

**Visual world:** Ivory field, a brass connection path, a large captured home-screen panel in a light browser frame, the product name as a small typographic signature, and a left-aligned editorial hook. A floating Wi-Fi chip reads “CONNECTED / Welcome in.” The capture is labeled as a sample guest experience.

**Choreography:** The screenshot panel slides and settles with a small perspective tilt; the hook and supporting line rise into place; a thin brass path traces toward the welcome panel and the connection chip appears.

**Techniques:** CSS 3D panel staging, kinetic editorial type, layered interface details.

**Transition:** 0.45s warm crossfade beginning at 3.60s, as the brand name starts in the voiceover.

**Depth:** BG ivory canvas and localized brass wash; MG captured interface; FG connection line and title.

### Beat 2 — A personal guide · 3.60–9.20s

**VO:** “Concierge.AI brings dining, directions, requests, and stay support into one personal guide.”

**Concept:** The same welcome unfolds into a living guest journey. Quick actions become helpful choices rather than a generic feature list, all grounded in labels present in the captured site.

**Visual world:** The captured interface anchors the left column while four quick-action cards — Explore dining, Find the pool, Request towels, and My Stay — cascade into a clean editorial layout. A bottom navigation strip echoes Home, Explore, Requests, My Stay, and Concierge.

**Choreography:** The interface card drifts left; four actions cascade with staggered entrances; a fine route line connects dining, directions, and requests; the active action receives one soft brass pulse.

**Techniques:** Staggered card cascade, SVG route drawing, restrained card-depth/parallax.

**Transition:** 0.45s warm crossfade beginning at 8.80s, carrying the route line into the operations panel.

**Depth:** BG ivory field and faint navigation ticks; MG real captured screen and action cards; FG route line and action labels.

### Beat 3 — In the team's hands · 8.80–14.00s

**VO:** “Hotel teams manage guest experience and operations with property-scoped, on-prem tools.”

**Concept:** Guest help now connects to the people who run the property. A sample request is routed into a calm operations board, showing the product's property-scoped workflows without implying a live customer deployment.

**Visual world:** A wide, light operations panel labeled “SAMPLE PROPERTY” and “ON-PREM” sits beside the guest action card. Rows show “Fresh towels → Housekeeping” and “Dining details → Property content”; no invented KPIs or response-time claims appear.

**Choreography:** The guest request card and operations panel arrive from opposite sides; the brass connector draws into the property boundary; the “ON-PREM” chip settles into view.

**Techniques:** Character typing for request details, SVG connector drawing, 3D panel settle.

**Transition:** 0.45s warm crossfade beginning at 13.70s, as the brand name returns in the voiceover.

**Depth:** BG ivory and thin floor-rule; MG operations board; FG property boundary, on-prem chip, and product label.

### Beat 4 — Hospitality, connected · 13.70–20.00s

**VO:** “Concierge.AI. Hospitality, connected.”

**Concept:** Guest, property team, and hotel systems meet around one clear product name. The close is spacious and confident, with time to read the brand rather than another feature list.

**Visual world:** The wordmark Concierge.AI anchors the left; three outlined nodes read “GUEST WI-FI,” “GUEST EXPERIENCE,” and “PROPERTY TEAM,” linked by one brass path. The final line reads “Hospitality, connected.”

**Choreography:** Nodes assemble on the path; the wordmark resolves from a gentle upward drift; the tagline appears last and holds through the final frame.

**Techniques:** SVG path drawing, kinetic type, grouped 3D-to-flat settle.

**Transition:** The prior beat crossfades into the brand close; the final frame holds without an exit.

**Depth:** BG ivory canvas; MG network path and nodes; FG wordmark and tagline.
