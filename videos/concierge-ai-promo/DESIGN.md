# Design System

## Overview

Concierge.AI's guest experience is warm, quiet, and hospitality-led. A spacious ivory canvas holds a centered white welcome panel, quick-action cards, a floating concierge composer, and a five-item guest navigation bar. Iowan Old Style gives the welcome message an editorial tone while Geist Sans keeps controls and supporting copy clear. Brass accents guide attention without making the interface feel ornamental.

## Colors

- **Canvas**: `#FAF8F4` — warm ivory background.
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` — guest panels, action cards, and composer.
- **Primary text**: `#1C1C1C` — headings and interface copy.
- **Secondary text**: `#6E6A64` — explanatory copy and quiet labels.
- **Accent**: `#B38A4A` — active navigation, eyebrow labels, and key actions.
- **Border**: `#E8E3DA` — soft card and panel edges.
- **Accent wash**: `#F7F2E9` — pale warm highlight.
- **Hospitality green**: `#174C43` — secondary operational accent, used sparingly.

## Typography

- **Display**: Iowan Old Style, weight 590; large, closely tracked hospitality headlines.
- **Interface and body**: Geist Sans, weights 400–700; labels, navigation, buttons, and descriptions.
- **Video scale**: headlines 72–104px, body 26–34px, labels 18–22px.

## Elevation

White cards sit above the ivory canvas with thin warm borders, 14–18px corners, and soft low-contrast shadows. Depth comes from overlapping interface panels and a few localized brass-tinted radial washes. Keep the overall frame light and open.

## Components

- **Guest welcome panel**: generous white card with an uppercase brass greeting, editorial headline, and one line of supporting copy.
- **Quick-action cards**: white rounded rectangles with serif labels for dining, directions, requests, and stay details.
- **Concierge composer**: pill-shaped white input with a circular brass send action.
- **Five-stop guest navigation**: Home, Explore, Requests, My Stay, and Concierge, with the active destination in brass.
- **Property operations panel**: restrained sample UI showing property-scoped content and service-request routing.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Keep the warm ivory, charcoal, and brass palette consistent across every beat.
- Let the serif headline carry the emotional message and use Geist Sans for product detail.
- Make the captured guest interface the visual source of truth.
- Use calm, deliberate motion with visible but controlled brass accents.

### Don'ts

- Do not introduce neon, dark-tech gradients, or generic AI imagery.
- Do not invent customer logos, hotel photography, performance metrics, or claims absent from the site.
- Do not present the sample property as a customer deployment.
- Do not create a separate logo mark; use the site title as a text wordmark.
