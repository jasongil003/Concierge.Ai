# Concierge.AI — 20-second product promo

**Audience:** Hotel operators and property teams  
**Message:** A hotel's Wi-Fi can be the first step into a smoother guest stay.  
**Angle:** Wi-Fi-to-welcome — turn the guest connection point into a personal guide, then show how property teams manage the experience on-prem.  
**Voice direction:** Warm, conversational, calm confidence; leave a short breath after the opening hook.

Your hotel's Wi-Fi can do more than connect. It can start a smoother stay.

Concierge.AI brings dining, directions, requests, and stay support into one personal guide.

Hotel teams manage guest experience and operations with property-scoped, on-prem tools.

Concierge.AI. Hospitality, connected.
