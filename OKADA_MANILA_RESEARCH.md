## Accommodation and guest information

The official Stay directory groups accommodation into rooms, suites, and villas. Listed rooms include Deluxe, Grand Deluxe, and Grand Deluxe Twin. Suite names include Junior, Premium, Executive, Garden, Manila Bay, Presidential, Senator, Celebrity, Governor, and Royal Maisonette. Villa names include Sky, Tower, El Nido, Bohol, and Boracay.

The May 2026 official resort directory lists in-room Wi-Fi, 24-hour in-room dining, walk-in closet, coffee and tea facilities, private bar, safe, writing desk, sitting area, TV and movie channels, an iPad-based room controller, intelligent toilet, and jet tub. Villa butler service is also listed. These are property-level published features, not guarantees for every room category; guests should confirm their booking details.

The official Stay deal pages publish check-in at 3:00 PM and check-out at 12:00 noon. Because the pages are offer-specific, treat these as a medium-confidence standard and confirm against a guest’s reservation. No rates are copied into this dataset.

## Guest FAQ answers suitable for a researched draft

- **Where can I eat Filipino food?** Kiapo serves Filipino cuisine on the Upper Ground Floor in Coral Wing. Confirm today’s hours with the official venue page.
- **Which restaurant serves Japanese cuisine?** Enbu, Kappou Yoshi, and Ginza Nagaoka are listed Japanese venues. Ginza Nagaoka’s hours conflict across official sources; do not state its hours without confirmation.
- **Where is Medley Buffet today?** Through 19 October 2026, the official notice places it at Sinag, 3rd Floor, Coral Wing. Its usual location is Upper Ground Floor, The Promenade, Pearl Wing. Check the official notice before answering after 19 October.
- **Where can my children play?** Okada Kids Club is listed on the Upper Ground Floor, Coral Wing. Sessions and age/supervision rules apply; confirm availability first.
- **Where is the nearest shopping mall?** Google Maps showed Ayala Malls Manila Bay about 2.3 km away by the displayed driving route; refresh the route for a current ETA.
- **How do I reach The Fountain / Enbu / the spa?** Use the researched map references to state Crystal Corridor / Crystal Pavilion, Upper Ground Floor Coral Wing, and 3rd Floor Pearl Wing respectively. Do not invent a route.
- **What time is check-out?** Official offer pages publish 12:00 noon; ask the guest to confirm their reservation terms.
- **Is The Fountain operating tonight?** The official page currently includes an undated adverse-weather notice saying the show is suspended tonight until further notice. Because the notice has no date, its validity cannot be established; do not promise operation and confirm with hotel staff.
