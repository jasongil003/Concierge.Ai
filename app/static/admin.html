Conversation modules

Create quick actions guests can tap in the concierge chat. Saving a module updates the guest menu immediately.

WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR

Give guests useful conversation shortcuts

Each module adds a named button to the guest chat menu. When a guest taps it, the saved prompt is sent as their chat message and the concierge responds using available property information and configured rules.

How to create a module

  1. Name it for the guest. Use a short action such as “Explore dining” or “Request extra towels.”
  2. Write the full prompt. Say what the guest wants help with; include useful constraints and ask the concierge to be clear when hotel details are unavailable.
  3. Set order and visibility. Lower order numbers appear first. Turn on “Show in guest menu” to make the action available to guests.
  4. Save and review. Saving applies the change immediately. Use “Open guest app” in the top bar to see the enabled menu actions.
Explore dining“Suggest restaurants that are open now and match my preferences.”
Room service“Show me the room-service options and how to place an order.”
Getting around“Help me find the best way to get to a nearby attraction.”

Keep information current: a module is a prompt shortcut, not a place to store hotel facts or trigger a backend workflow. Add verified hotel details in Hotel Information or Knowledge so the concierge has reliable facts to answer with.

Personalization

Control which guest memory and recommendation features are available at this property. Guests still choose whether to opt in.

WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR

Set privacy rules for tailored guest assistance

Choose which personalization options the concierge may offer, what can be remembered after a guest agrees, and which context sources may inform recommendations.

How to use these settings

  1. Choose availability and a suggested level. The default is shown to guests as a starting point; it never opts them in automatically.
  2. Set learning and expiry. Decide whether eligible preferences may be learned after opt-in, and how long saved preferences remain available.
  3. Choose allowed context. Enable only the PMS, location, and internet sources your property has configured and approved.
  4. Save settings. Guests can still switch to a more private level, review or remove preferences, and turn personalization off.

WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR

Manage guest sessions and temporary stay records

Reconnect a WLAN device to group its visits under a time-limited stay, then review the linked sessions and property-scoped device identity. Guest replies use only authenticated, linked context allowed by the guest's personalization settings.
  1. 1. ReconnectUse an authorized WLAN MAC and optional room or integration IDs to create or restore a stay.
  2. 2. Add stay notesSelect an active stay to save a concise summary, preferences, request notes, and important context for staff reference.

Privacy: the raw WLAN MAC is converted server-side to a property-scoped pseudonymous device ID. Stay notes are retained with the stay and are not automatically included in guest replies; avoid passwords, payment data, or unnecessary sensitive details.

Facilities

Manage guest-facing details and operating status for shared amenities such as the pool, gym, spa, parking, and business center. Restaurants are managed separately.

WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR

Keep amenity information accurate for guests and staff

Facility records provide the concierge with names, locations, hours, descriptions, and operating status when guests ask about shared property amenities. Some facility types also support quick answers for pool, gym, spa, parking, and business center questions.

How to use this page

  1. Add one record per amenity. Use a clear name and a recognizable type such as pool, gym, spa, parking, or business center.
  2. Enter guest-ready details. Add where it is, its usual hours, and practical directions or access information in Description.
  3. Keep availability current. Open means available; Full means at capacity. Closed or Temporarily closed can be reported when a guest asks. Maintenance and Private event records are omitted from the guest facilities list.
  4. Review changes. Edit or search the table, then ask the concierge about the amenity or open the guest app to review the response.
Use this pagePool, gym, spa, parking, business center, and other shared amenities.
Use RestaurantsDining venues, menus, meal periods, reservations, and promotions.
Use the floor mapMap positions, zones, and navigation. A facility record alone does not place an item on the map.

Guest visibility: these records are separate from PMS inventory. Do not include staff-only codes, private contact details, or internal procedures in guest-facing descriptions. A saved facility may also be referenced by a restaurant or property event.
